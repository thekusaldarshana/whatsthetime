We’re excited to share an important update as we step into **2026** 🎉  

**What’s The Time has officially moved to a new domain:  
👉 https://whatsthetime.top**

---

## Why the change?

This move is part of our long-term vision to make What’s The Time faster, cleaner, and more future-ready. The new `.top` domain gives us better flexibility as we expand tools, content, and global features throughout 2026 and beyond.

---

## What happens to whatsthetime.online?

Nothing breaks.  
All traffic from **whatsthetime.online** is now **fully redirected** to **whatsthetime.top**, so:

- Your bookmarks still work  
- Old links still work  
- Search engine rankings remain intact  

You’ll simply land on the new domain automatically.

---

## What stays the same?

Everything you already use and love:

- The **Time Converter** (now at `/`)
- The **Meeting Planner** (at `/meeting-planner`)
- All existing tools, blogs, and features
- The same focus on speed, simplicity, and accuracy

Only the address bar changes.

---

## What’s next?

2026 is a big year for What’s The Time. Expect:
- Smarter global time tools  
- Better performance  
- More practical content for remote work and global collaboration  

This domain change is the first step.
